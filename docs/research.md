## Current vendor distinctions

[Fiddler pricing](https://www.fiddler.ai/pricing) shows Developer usage priced per trace, with Enterprise through sales. Do not confuse this with the separate five-model SageMaker package. The page uses words for current main-site pricing and labels the marketplace example separately. This build is a focused regression-monitoring desk, not a replacement for the vendor's complete platform.

[Fiddler's model reference](https://docs.fiddler.ai/sdk-api/python-client/model#download_data) documents CSV observations export, configurable columns, a default row cap and explicit environments. The importer uses a manifest because headers are model-specific. The vendor's [model drift guide](https://docs.fiddler.ai/reference/glossary/model-drift) explains separate distribution and performance signals; the free desk's formulas and limits are independently documented in metrics.md.

The differentiating workflow combines a business-defined numerical comparison, missing-outcome evidence, saved review results and locally assigned follow-ups. Do not claim Fiddler lacks those capabilities. The offer is ownership and customisation, not invented feature superiority or guaranteed savings.
